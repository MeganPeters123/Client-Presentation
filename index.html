AUM History

Trade Records

Date, security, buy/sell, quantity, price, value — add one or more files, each can have its own column layout (e.g. separate broker/platform reports)

Fund Holdings

Position-level valuation exports, one file per fund — format is auto-detected, no mapping needed. Add as many funds as you have.

SA Revenue Split

The analyst workbook's "SA Income Categories" tab — ticker plus % of revenue earned in SA. Drives the look-through allocation.

GICS Sectors

"GICS.xlsx" — ticker, SEDOL and GICS sector per holding. Drives the sector split.

Index Weights

"Index Weights - Month End.csv" — month-end constituent weights per index. Drives active share.
